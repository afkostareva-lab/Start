Образцы для письменных заданий модуля «Profiles»: письмо другу, абзац о книжном герое, статья о человеке, которым восхищаешься, письмо о достопримечательности России и короткий текст по истории. Жирным выделено то, что можно заменить своим.

5 образцов
Email о подростке, который тебя вдохновляет (3a, 90 слов)

Hi Tom,

How are you? I've just read an article about some amazing teenagers and I want to tell you about Olivia Hallisey. She is from the USA. When she heard about the Ebola disaster in Africa, she invented a cheap paper test which changes colour when it detects the virus. She won the main award at Google's Science Fair.

Olivia is hard-working, creative and determined. I find her interesting because she used science to help people in need. I'd like to be like her one day!

Who do you admire? Write back soon,
Masha

Структура: приветствие → кто и откуда → что делает → черты характера → почему интересен → прощание.

Абзац о любимом книжном персонаже (3b, 90 слов)

My favourite character is Hermione Granger from the Harry Potter books by J.K. Rowling. She is a slim teenage girl of medium height with long, curly brown hair and big brown eyes. She usually wears her black school robes and carries lots of heavy books. Hermione is clever, hard-working and brave. She always helps her friends, Harry and Ron, even when it is dangerous. What is special about her is that she never gives up and believes that knowledge can solve any problem. That's why I like her so much.

План: name → appearance → clothes → character → what is special about him/her.

Статья «The person I admire» (3c, 80–100 слов)

Para 1: The person that I admire most is Yuri Gagarin. He was born on 9th March 1934 in Klushino, near Smolensk. He is famous for being the first man in space.
Para 2: When he was young, he lived through the war and his village was occupied.
Para 3: Some years later, he became a pilot. On 12th April 1961, he flew around the Earth in Vostok 1. Sadly, he died in a plane crash in 1968.
Para 4: The reason I admire him is because he was brave and determined. When I think of him, I know that dreams can come true.

Абзацы: 1 — имя, дата и место рождения, чем знаменит · 2 — ранние годы · 3 — поздние годы · 4 — почему восхищаешься. Другие идеи: Валентина Терешкова, Дмитрий Менделеев, Алексей Маресьев.

Email о достопримечательности России с людьми в форме (3d, 90 слов)

Hi Kate,

Thanks for your email about the Beefeaters! We have something similar in Moscow. If you visit the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier near the Kremlin wall, you can see the guards of the Presidential Regiment. They are tall young soldiers and they wear dark uniforms and white gloves and carry rifles. They stand completely still for an hour. Then they change the guard and march in a very special way. It looks amazing and crowds of tourists come to watch it.

Come to Moscow and see it with me!
Best wishes,
Masha

Включи: where it is · who you can see there · what they look like · what they do there.

Short text: дети-работники в России XIX века (Extensive Reading)

In the 19th century many children in Russia had to work. In the villages they looked after animals and helped their parents in the fields. In the cities they worked in factories, shops and workshops from the age of eight or ten. They worked 12–14 hours a day for very low wages. Many children were apprentices: they lived with their masters, who were often cruel. Their lives were hard, and most of them never went to school. In 1882 a new law stopped children under 12 from working in factories.

Расскажи: what kind of jobs they did · working hours and wages · what their lives were like.
